## 3. The window's drag strip, and selection

**Cause of the drag.** `.app` is the drag region and `.app > *` makes every child no-drag. The
tile layer (`TileLayer.tsx`, the box the tile webviews are placed in) is a child of `.app` and is
`position: fixed; inset: 0`, so it was a no-drag rectangle over the whole window, top bar
included. A press in the bar then reached the page and started a text selection. Found by
measuring: `-webkit-app-region` IS inherited in Chromium (checked in a bare Electron 44 window:
children of a drag element compute `drag`), and the keyword `none` is not accepted; `initial` is.

- `styles.css` (new block at the end): `.app > .tile-layer { -webkit-app-region: initial }`, so the
  layer and the pages in it are no region at all; the pages lie over the columns, which are
  no-drag. `.app > .topbar` drags between its controls and `.topbar > *` do not (deck's rule), so
  the other agent's new button there is covered without a change.
- **Selection** (same block): `body` is `user-select: none`; text is selectable in a conversation
  (`.chat`), the preview pane's body, inputs, textareas and contenteditable. xterm handles its
  own. A press in one conversation (`main.tsx`, a capture listener) marks it and puts
  `ws-selecting` on the root until the button comes up; while it is there every other `.chat` is
  `user-select: none`, so a drag cannot run its selection on into the tiles below.
- Smoke (`src/main/smoke-ui.ts`): every element's computed `-webkit-app-region` laid over each
  other in document order, as Electron builds the window's region (drag adds, no-drag takes
  away), sampled every 12 px across the bar at y 44 and every 40 px at y 4 and y 80: the empty bar
  is all drag, the bar's controls all no-drag, the center column no-drag, the tile layer `none`,
  and `elementFromPoint` in the empty bar is `.app` itself. Computed `user-select` on the body, the
  bar, both kinds of tile head, the launcher and the +: none; on a conversation, its prose, the
  prompt bar and the launcher's field: text. A mousedown in one conversation makes the other
  `none`, mouseup gives it back.
- **Needs a real pointer:** the smoke window is hidden, so nobody has dragged the window or
  selected across tiles with a mouse yet. Colin: drag by the empty bar, by the Knot at the top
  left, and between the bar's buttons; drag-select in a conversation down into the next tile.

## 5. Dropping images on a session

Read: deck's `lib/drop.ts`, `useDropTarget.tsx`, its preload's `keepDroppedFile`, the window-level
guard in its `main.tsx`, `Tile.tsx` and `FocusPane.tsx`.

**Likely cause of "doesn't work".** A session tile took a drop, but only as a chip waiting in its
prompt bar for ⏎; the focused session's terminal, the biggest target, took no drop at all, and
nothing stopped an unclaimed file drop from trying to navigate the window.

- `src/renderer/src/drop.ts` (after deck): `hasFiles`, `dropEffectFor` (claims an effect the
  source allows, or a screenshot thumbnail's copy-only drag never fires), `keptPaths` (through
  `keepDrop` and main's `drops.ts`: a Finder file's own path, a copy under `<profile>/drops/` for
  a temp file, a picture's bytes from a browser or another app written there), `pastePaths` (one
  bracketed paste, shell-escaped, as a terminal does) and `useFileDrop` (counted enter/leave, a
  non-file drag passed on to the column's reorder).
- Where a drop lands: the terminal in the center (`FocusPane`) pastes the paths into it; a
  session tile's conversation or head pastes into that session and brings it into focus (deck's
  Tile); a tile's prompt bar keeps it as a chip, sent with the text on ⏎ (track E's flow).
- `main.tsx`: a file dragged over or dropped anywhere else is `preventDefault`ed (only file
  drags, so text still drops into fields). The tile webviews let a file drag through only during a
  reorder; a Finder drag over a tile page goes to that page, which has no file access (sandbox).
- Not handled: a browser drag that carries only a URL and no file (no `Files` type). deck does not
  either; fetching it would put the network in core's window.
- Smoke: synthetic `DataTransfer` drops of a PNG on a tile's prompt bar (a chip, the launcher
  still in the center; sent, the stand-in CLI's screen shows the `drops/` path), on another
  tile's conversation (pasted into that session, which comes into focus with its terminal), on the
  terminal host (pasted), and on the top bar (claimed, the window stays on its page).
- **Needs a real pointer:** a real Finder drag, a screenshot thumbnail, and an image dragged out
  of a browser, onto each of the three.

## 6. The wheel over a tile

**Cause.** deck's rule is that a tile never eats the wheel. In workshop each tile is a webview laid
over the column, so the wheel went into the page and never reached `.tile-scroll`; only core's tile
heads let it through.

- `src/preload/tile.ts`: a passive `wheel` listener. If nothing from the target up (or the page's
  root) can scroll that way, or what can is at its end, the delta (lines and pages turned into px)
  is summed and sent once per animation frame (a 50 ms timer covers a page that is not drawing).
  Not sent: a pinch (`ctrlKey`), a mostly sideways wheel, a wheel the page cancelled (checked when
  the frame flushes), and anything outside `tile` view.
- `src/main/index.ts` (`CH.wheel`): checked like every bridge call (`guestOf`: a live tile, its
  top frame, its own origin), only in `tile` view, a finite number clamped to ±4000, then
  `CH.tileWheel` to core's window. `App.tsx`: the tile column's `scrollTop += dy`. No smoothing
  is added: a trackpad's deltas (momentum included) already are the motion, one step a frame.
- `template/CLAUDE.md`, "Rules of the sandbox": the column takes the wheel at the page's edge;
  `preventDefault` keeps it; pinch is never passed; pane and overlay keep every wheel.
- The session column: its conversations chain to the column at their ends (section 4).
- Smoke: a wheel over Wikipedia (nothing to scroll) scrolls the column; a ctrl-wheel does not; a
  wheel in a scrolling box in the probe page stays in the page, and at the box's (and page's) end
  the column takes it.
- **Needs a real pointer:** a trackpad flick over a tile, to feel the momentum hand-off.
