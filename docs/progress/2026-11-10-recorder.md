## 1. How deck's recorder works

Read end to end in `~/deck`: `src/main/recorder.ts`, `src/preload/recorder.ts`,
`src/renderer/recorder.html`, `src/renderer/src/recorder/main.tsx` and `recorder.css`,
`plugin/native/recaudio.swift`, the wiring in `src/main/index.ts` and `menu.ts`, the top bar's
button in `App.tsx`, `build/entitlements.mac.plist` and `package.json`'s `extendInfo`.

- **Windows.** The recorder is a window of its own: frameless, transparent, no shadow, always on
  top (`floating`), on every Space and over full-screen apps, not resizable by macOS, kept out of
  captures with `setContentProtection(true)`. It is a 14 px border (the band you drag by) around
  the filmed rectangle, and a 46 px toolbar under it. It opens centred on the display of deck's
  window, the filmed rectangle 80% of the work area's height (420 px at least).
- **Click-through.** The window starts with `setIgnoreMouseEvents(true, { forward: true })`. The
  page watches `mousemove` and asks main to stop ignoring the pointer when it is over the border
  or the bar, and to ignore it again over the inside, so the app under the frame is used as usual.
- **Dragging and sizing** are done by the page, not by `-webkit-app-region` (which would swallow
  the pointer events the click-through needs): pointer capture on the border or bar sends the
  window's new top-left corner to main (`setPosition`); the corner grip sends a new filmed height
  (whichever way the pointer went further, the width following at 9:16), and main clamps it
  between 420 and the display and keeps the top-left corner. Coordinates go through `| 0`,
  because `Math.round(-0.3)` is `-0` and Electron's `setPosition` throws on it.
- **Capture.** `desktopCapturer.getSources({ types: ['screen'] })` in main, picking the display
  under the frame's middle; the page opens it with the legacy
  `getUserMedia({ video: { mandatory: { chromeMediaSource: 'desktop', chromeMediaSourceId } } })`
  at the display's full pixel size, up to 60 fps. Not `getDisplayMedia`, and not ScreenCaptureKit
  directly (Chromium uses it underneath on recent macOS). Every 1/30 s, on a timer (a still screen
  sends no frames, and the frame may be moving), the page draws the part of the video under the
  frame's inside NOW into a 1080 × 1920 canvas, so dragging the frame while recording pans the shot.
- **The Mac's sound** goes through BlackHole 2ch, a virtual audio device the user installs
  (`brew install blackhole-2ch`). A small Swift program, `recaudio`, compiled with `swiftc` into
  the profile the first time it is needed (so Xcode's command line tools are required), makes a
  stacked multi-output device "<current output> + Record" (the current output leading, BlackHole
  drift-corrected), switches the default output to it, and later switches back. You keep hearing
  everything; BlackHole gets a copy, which the page records as an ordinary audio input. The output
  is put back at the stop, on close, and on quit (synchronously in `will-quit`). Recording any
  input needs the microphone permission, so main asks for it first and opens the Microphone pane
  of System Settings after a refusal. The microphone itself can be mixed in (never BlackHole as
  "the microphone").
- **Encoding.** `canvas.captureStream(30)` plus the mixed audio track (a Web Audio destination) into
  `MediaRecorder`, preferring H.264 in MP4 (the hardware encoder), else WebM, at 20 Mb/s and 256 kb/s,
  in one-second chunks streamed to main over IPC and appended to `take.<ext>` in the profile. At the
  stop, if `ffmpeg` is on the PATH, it is made what Instagram asks for (H.264 High 4.2, yuv420p,
  constant 30 fps, 1080 × 1920, AAC 48 kHz stereo, `+faststart`); otherwise the raw take is moved.
- **Where files go.** `~/Movies/Foxtrot's Workshop/deck <date> <time>.mp4`, a silent notification
  that reveals it in Finder on click, and Play / Show in Finder buttons in the bar.
- **The bar.** Record (a 3, 2, 1 countdown in the frame first; the button shows the elapsed time and
  pulses while recording), the Mac's sound on/off, the microphone on/off, Instagram's safe-zone
  guides on/off (hidden while filming), Play and Show in Finder after a save, close (asking first
  mid-take), the resize grip. A warning replaces the bar until clicked. The switches were in the
  page's `localStorage`.
- **Shortcuts.** ⌘⇧O, as a menu accelerator ("Screen Recorder (Reels, 9:16)"): opens the frame;
  while it is up, stops a take (or cancels the countdown), else closes it. The top bar's button
  does the same, titled "Screen recorder: a 9:16 frame for Reels / Stories you drag anywhere, with
  the Mac's sound (⌘⇧O)".
- **Entitlements and Info.plist.** `com.apple.security.device.audio-input` (under the hardened
  runtime, macOS silently refuses any audio input without it, BlackHole included) and
  `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`. Screen capture needs no key: macOS's Screen Recording permission
  is granted in System Settings, and deck opens that pane when it is off.
- **Not carried over:** the PS2 and "Foxtrot Plays" panes laying the frame over their stage
  (`Recorder.cover`, `ps2Record`), the `rec:phase` feed to deck's body pad, and the phone stub.

- **Core, not a tile.** The frame's page runs on its own in-memory partition (`workshop-recorder`),
  so the permission handlers set for it touch nothing else: `media` is granted only to the
  frame's own webContents, devices and the display-media picker are refused, every request other
  than `file:`, `data:`, `blob:` and the dev server is cancelled, downloads are refused. Every
  `ws:rec:*` channel answers only the frame's main frame; the two core-window channels answer only
  core's window. `main/tiles.ts` is not touched: tiles still get no screen capture and no display
  media (smoke checks it with the frame open). The frame's preload is sandboxed (deck's was not)
  and exposes only `window.rec`, never `window.core`. `reveal` and `play` act only on a file inside
  the takes folder (resolved, not a string prefix as in deck).
- **Theme and fonts.** No colour of deck's is left: the border band is the theme's panel at 60%
  (never fully clear, or macOS lets clicks through it), the bar the panel, the edge the accent, and
  "recording" the theme's red, `--ws-blocked`. Main hands the frame the same `--ws-*` variables a
  tile gets (fonts included) at load and on every theme change. Nunito is loaded from
  `assets/fonts` like core's window.
- **Icons.** deck used `lucide-react`, which workshop does not have. Rather than add a dependency,
  the bar's twelve glyphs are drawn inline as small SVG paths written for this (MIT, ours).
- **Shortcut.** ⌘⇧O was free. `isRecorderKey` in `src/shared/shortcuts.ts` (beside `shortcutOf`,
  whose type is unchanged, so the window's handler is untouched). Main acts on it itself in the
  same `before-input-event` hook as the others, so it works from core's window, any tile's page
  (the page never sees it) and the frame itself. workshop has no app menu, so no accelerator.
- **Settings.** The three switches are `settings.recorder` (`{ sound, mic, guides }`) in core's
  settings, cleaned field by field and absent until first changed, instead of the page's
  `localStorage`.
- **Files.** Takes land in `~/Movies/workshop/` as `workshop 2026-11-10 14.03.22.mp4`; a second
  take in the same second gets ` 2`. A smoke run saves into its throwaway profile's `recordings/`.
- **The top bar's button** (`RecordButton.tsx`, beside the usage meters; CSS under "Recorder" at
  the end of `styles.css`): "Record" with a hollow dot at rest; a filled dot while the frame is up;
  while a take runs, the theme's red, a pulsing dot and the elapsed time (`0:12`), titled "Recording:
  click or ⌘⇧O to stop and save". At rest its title is deck's. A click does what ⌘⇧O does.
  The bar's height and the Knot are not touched.
- **Every Space, not over others' full screen** (fixed after use, 10 Nov 2026). deck's
  `setVisibleOnAllWorkspaces(true, { visibleOnFullScreen: true })` makes Electron call
  `app.dock.hide()`, so while the frame was up workshop was a background app: no Dock icon, no menu
  bar, out of ⌘Tab, its window lost behind others. Now `skipTransformProcessType: true` (checked:
  `app.dock.isVisible()` stays true). The cost: the frame may no longer float over another app's
  full-screen Space.

## 7. For Colin

- **Packaging.** workshop has no electron-builder config yet. When it does, it needs
  `com.apple.security.device.audio-input` in the entitlements and an
  `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` such as "The screen recorder records the Mac's sound (through
  BlackHole) and, if you ask, your microphone." `native/` must ship with the app (main reads
  `native/recaudio.swift` from the app path).
- **A possible later simplification, not done:** recent Electron can give system audio directly
  (`setDisplayMediaRequestHandler` with `audio: 'loopback'`, on macOS through ScreenCaptureKit /
  Core Audio taps), which would remove BlackHole, the Swift helper and the need for Xcode's tools.
  It would change how the recorder works and cannot be checked without a real capture, so it is
  left for a decision.
- **A question about the tile sandbox, not caused by this work:** `main/tiles.ts` grants `media`
  with `mediaTypes: ['video']` to any tile that declares `camera`. Whether a legacy
  `getUserMedia({ video: { mandatory: { chromeMediaSource: 'desktop' } } })` from such a tile
  arrives as a plain `video` request, and whether it can capture without a source id from
  `desktopCapturer`, was not checked (the probe tile has no camera permission). Worth a smoke check
  with a camera-permitted probe.
- **Visual check.** The button's look in the top bar was checked only by its classes in smoke; the
  agent resizing the top bar may want to eyeball it beside the meters.
