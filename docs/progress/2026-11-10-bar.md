## The choice: at rest

"The knot takes up the same height as foxtrot": Foxtrot's height is his standing height, 15 sheet
pixels, which he has in every pose but sleep (60 px in the bar, 15 in a head). The Knot now has
that height **at rest**, because rest is how it is seen most (every session waiting for you, or
none open), and a resting knot of 22 px against a 60 px fox was the gap Colin saw.

Matching it while working instead would have left the resting mark at 37 px in the bar and
shrunk the working knot in a head. Matching both is not possible while size still says "working".

At rest it cannot be both 60 in an 88 bar and much smaller than the working knot: the working
knot must fit the bar (a flare fills the whole box). So the rest size went from 0.62 to 0.8 of
full, which is deck's own swing (Foxtrot is 44 asleep and 56–60 awake, 0.73–0.79), and needs and
start moved up with it to keep the order work > needs > start > rest. Speed (22 s against 2.4 s a
loop) and colour still carry most of the status. In a session head the box stays 24, so the head
does not grow, and the resting knot is his 15 px exactly; in the bar it is 55.7 on average,
turning between 50 and 60, against his 56 running and 60 standing.
